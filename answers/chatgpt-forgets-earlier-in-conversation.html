Why does ChatGPT forget what I told it earlier in the same chat?

Short answer: ChatGPT forgets earlier parts of a long chat because it can only pay close attention to a limited amount of text at once, and the oldest messages get the least attention. The fix is to keep a short summary of key facts and decisions outside the chat, start new chats more often, and paste that summary in at the start of each one.

Why this happens

ChatGPT does not remember a conversation the way a person does. Every time you send a message, it rereads the conversation. When the conversation gets very long, early details get less attention or drop out entirely.

This is why a chat can feel sharp at the start and then begin asking you to paste things again, or confidently contradict a decision you made earlier.

Memory and custom instructions help with things you want it to always know about you, but they are not a full record of a long project.

How to fix it, step by step

  1. Keep a short working notes document with your goal, key facts, and decisions you have made.
  2. When a chat starts slipping, ask ChatGPT for a handoff summary covering the goal, current state, decisions, and next step.
  3. Read the summary and fix anything it got wrong.
  4. Open a new chat and paste the summary as your first message, then tell it the one next task.
  5. Put permanent preferences, like your tone of voice, in custom instructions or a project so you do not have to repeat them every time.
  6. Start a fresh chat for each major piece of work rather than running one endless thread.

Common questions

Is ChatGPT forgetting things a bug?

Usually not. It is a normal limit of how these models handle long conversations. Shorter chats with a pasted summary work around it.

Does ChatGPT memory fix this?

Memory helps ChatGPT remember general facts about you across chats. It is not designed to hold every detail of a long project, so a written summary is still the safer approach.

How long is too long for one chat?

There is no single cutoff. A good rule is to start a new chat whenever you notice it repeating questions, forgetting decisions, or drifting from your style.

Will a new chat lose all my work?

Not if you carry a summary over. A clear handoff summary lets the new chat pick up where the old one left off.
